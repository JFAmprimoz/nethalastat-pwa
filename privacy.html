Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

We believe in privacy by default. This Privacy Policy explains how our Progressive Web App (PWA) handles your data. The short version: we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

1. Local Storage (Your Data Stays With You)

Our app operates entirely on your device. Any preferences, settings, or app states are saved locally on your device using your web browser's Local Storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers, and we do not have access to it. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, your local app data will be permanently deleted.

2. Analytics (Cookieless Tracking)

To understand how our app is used and to improve the user experience, we use Umami Analytics. Umami is a privacy-first, open-source analytics platform.

  • No Cookies: Umami does not use tracking cookies.
  • No Personal Data: It does not collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like your name, email, or IP address.
  • Anonymized Data: It only tracks basic, aggregated usage patterns (such as page views, device type, and country).

3. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Marketing

Our app contains links to third-party websites, including affiliate links. Please note that we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you for purchases made after clicking these affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
